## THE PROBLEM

Enterprise architecture capability assessments are essential for IT modernization but prohibitively expensive and slow. State governments and large enterprises spend **$500,000–$750,000** and wait **6–8 months** for comprehensive EA assessments. This timeline renders results outdated before implementation begins, particularly critical as organizations rush to adopt cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and zero-trust security architectures.

**We know this firsthand.** The founder recently served as Enterprise Architect on VDOT's Highway Maintenance Management System (HMMS) assessment—an 8-month, 6-person engagement that produced a 115-page report with 40 findings and 45 recommendations. The estimated cost: **$717,500**.

The root cause is labor-intensive manual analysis: reviewing hundreds of documents, conducting dozens of stakeholder interviews, classifying support tickets, administering surveys, performing complex gap analyses, and synthesizing transformation roadmaps. These tasks require scarce specialized expertise (TOGAF-certified architects earning $150,000–$250,000 annually), creating bottlenecks that limit assessment frequency.

Virginia state agencies face these challenges acutely. With limited IT budgets and modernization mandates (Executive Order 19, Commonwealth Digital Service initiatives), agencies must make data-driven decisions about **$2.1 billion in annual IT spending**. Yet traditional assessment costs consume 5–10% of transformation budgets before any actual modernization begins.

---

## OUR SOLUTION

Xenon Enterprise Consulting is building an AI-driven platform that automates enterprise architecture capability assessments, reducing analysis time from **8 months to 3 months** and cutting costs by **75%**. The platform employs a multi-agent AI architecture that:

1. **Automates document analysis** — AI agents classify, chunk, and extract entities from policies, architecture diagrams, and system inventories in hours, not weeks

2. **Deploys specialized assessment crews** — Intake, Discovery, Assessment, and Reporting agents work in coordinated multi-agent workflows using LangGraph and CrewAI

3. **Applies consistent maturity scoring** — AI evaluates capabilities against CMM frameworks with standardized criteria, eliminating assessor variability

4. **Integrates institutional knowledge** — Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) ensures assessments reflect organizational context from all ingested documents

5. **Generates draft reports automatically** — Structured findings, gap analyses, and recommendations produced in hours instead of weeks

### Why Open Source LLM?
- **Data privacy:** All processing on-premises/private cloud
- **Government compliance:** No data sent to external APIs
- **Cost control:** No per-token charges at scale
- **Customization:** Fine-tuning on EA domain data

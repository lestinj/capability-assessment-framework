## Option 1: VIPC Launch Grant (RECOMMENDED FIRST STEP)

### Overview
- **Amount:** $50,000 grant
- **Match Required:** 1:1 (non-dilutive, can be in-kind)
- **Timeline:** 60 days from application period opening to funding
- **Application Cycle:** Quarterly (highly competitive, ~20 grants/year total)
- **Funding:** Single tranche

### Eligibility Requirements

**Company Requirements:**
- [ ] For-profit Virginia-based company
- [ ] Headquartered in Virginia
- [ ] Administrative and strategic operations in Virginia
- [ ] Registered with Virginia SCC (in good standing)
- [ ] 50%+ of senior management are Virginia residents
- [ ] 50%+ of founders' ownership held by Virginia residents
- [ ] Valid federal tax ID (EIN)
- [ ] Not debarred on SAM.gov

**Stage Requirements:**
- [ ] Pre-MVP (no minimum viable product yet)
- [ ] No product revenue
- [ ] No prior Launch, CCF, or CRCF awards
- [ ] No capital from investors (up to $300K from family/friends OK if raised 90+ days prior)

**Industry Requirements (must be in one):**
- [x] IT (includes data science and analytics) ← **YOU QUALIFY**
- [ ] Advanced manufacturing
- [ ] Aerospace
- [ ] Cybersecurity and cyber-physical systems
- [ ] Life and health sciences
- [ ] Others listed in terms

### Matching Funds Requirements

You need $50,000 in matching funds. Acceptable sources:
- ✅ In-kind contributions (your time valued at fair market rate)
- ✅ Federal grants (NSF SBIR if received)
- ✅ Foundation grants
- ✅ Private grants
- ✅ Non-dilutive capital
- ❌ Virginia state funds (cannot use as match)
- ❌ Angel/VC investment

**Your Match Strategy:**
| Source | Amount | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Founder time (in-kind) | $40,000+ | 400+ hrs @ $100/hr |
| Consulting revenue | $10,000+ | If available |
| **Total** | **$50,000** | Meets 1:1 requirement |

### Application Components

Based on the terms and conditions, you'll need:

1. **Company Information**
   - Legal business name
   - Virginia SCC Entity ID
   - Federal Tax ID (EIN)
   - SAM.gov UEI
   - Primary business address
   - Senior management residency info

2. **Project Description**
   - Technology overview
   - Market opportunity
   - Competitive landscape
   - Commercialization plan

3. **Budget**
   - Sources and uses of funds
   - Matching funds identification
   - 6-12 month project timeline

4. **Team Information**
   - Founder background
   - Key personnel
   - Advisory board (if any)

### Post-Award Requirements

- Quarterly 4-question surveys for 36 months
- VIPC retains preemptive right to purchase up to 10% in next financing round ($500K+)
- Must remain Virginia-based for 3 years (or repay grant)
- Financial/records audit possible for 3 years post-award

### Application Timing

VIPC accepts applications quarterly. Check the current cycle deadline at:
https://vipc.org/funding/virginia-venture-partners/

**Pro Tip:** Attend "Coffee with VVP" (virtual, every other Friday at 9am) for Q&A before applying.

## Option 2: CCF Private Sector Grant

### Overview
- **Amount:** Up to $75,000
- **Match Required:** 1:1
- **Timeline:** ~90 days turnaround
- **Status:** Was paused in late 2024, expected to relaunch in 2025

### Eligibility (Similar to Launch Grant)
- For-profit Virginia-based company
- Good standing with Virginia SCC and federal government
- Operating in targeted industry (IT qualifies)
- No more than one prior CCF/CRCF grant
- Received no more than $1M from investors

### Key Differences from Launch Grant
- Allows companies further along (not strictly pre-MVP)
- Higher funding ceiling ($75K vs $50K)
- Allows some prior investor funding (up to $1M)
- Rolling applications (not quarterly cycles)

### Contact for Status
Check if this program has relaunched:
- Email: ccf@VirginiaIPC.org
- Website: https://vipc.org/funding/startups/

---

## Option 3: Launch Note (After Launch Grant)

### Overview
- **Amount:** Up to $150,000 (convertible note)
- **Match Required:** 2:1 (must include capital from investment closed within 90 days)
- **Terms:** Uncapped note, 20% discount, 4% interest, Virginia side letter (3-year HQ requirement)

### Eligibility
- Previous Launch Grant recipients preferred
- Must be actively raising $300K-$750K

### When to Apply
- After successfully executing Launch Grant
- When you have investor interest/term sheets
